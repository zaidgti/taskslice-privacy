Privacy Policy for TaskSlice

Last updated: September 26, 2026

TaskSlice ("the app") is developed and operated by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Account information. You can try TaskSlice as a guest, in which case an anonymous account ID is created for you and no personal information is collected. You can also sign in with an email address and password, or your Google account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo as provided by Google. This is used only to identify your account.

Task content. Text you enter to describe a task is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a step-by-step breakdown. This text is processed by Google to generate the response and is not stored by us beyond what's needed to complete that request.

Usage data for the free daily limit. To enforce a free daily limit, our server stores your account ID, a randomly generated device identifier, the date, and a count of how many breakdowns you've requested. This is used only for quota enforcement, is not linked to any other personal information, and is automatically deleted after 7 days.

Your projects and progress. Your projects, task breakdowns, and completion status are stored locally on your device. If you have a Pro account, they are also synced to Google Cloud Firestore under your account so they're available on your other devices; only you can access that copy. For everyone else, this data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers except for the one-time processing described above.

Voice input. If you use the microphone button to describe a task, the speech is handled by your device's own speech-recognition service (for example, Google's), under that provider's privacy policy. TaskSlice only receives the resulting text, which is then treated like typed task content.

Text shared into the app. If you use "Share to TaskSlice" from another app, the shared text is placed into a new task for you to review; nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to create the breakdown.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep your data separate from other users
  • To enforce the free-tier daily usage limit
  • To generate task breakdowns via Google's Gemini API

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as described above.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — handles sign-in
  • Google Gemini API — processes the text of tasks you submit to generate breakdowns
  • Google Cloud Firestore — stores synced projects for Pro accounts only
  • Google Fonts — the app downloads its typeface from Google's font servers, which receive your device's IP address as part of that request

All are subject to Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention and Deletion

Your local project data stays on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Signing out of a guest session deletes that guest's projects from the device.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: tap the account icon on the home screen, then Delete account. After you confirm your password (or Google account), this permanently deletes your account, your usage records on our server, any projects synced for a Pro account, and all TaskSlice data on that device. The only thing kept is the anonymous per-device daily counter described above, which isn't linked to your account and is deleted automatically within 7 days. If you no longer have the app installed, you can instead email us at the address below to request deletion, and we'll delete your account and data manually.

Children's Privacy

TaskSlice is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
